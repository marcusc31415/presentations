Let $G$ be a (P)-closed group and $\Delta = (\Gamma, (X_a), (G(v)))$ its associated local action diagram. Then $G$ is unimodular if and only if for all cycles $C$ in $\Gamma$ we have that $\prod_{a \in C}|X_a| = \prod_{a \in C}|X_{\overline{a}}|$.
\end{center}
